Notice the plan trade didn't hit a clean 2R — real fills rarely land on round numbers. That's the honest point: the win doesn't need to be dramatic. What matters is that its downside was fixed at ₹4,995 before the trade was ever placed, while the averaging trade's downside was never fixed at all — and on a bigger down day than this one, that's the difference between a small write-off and the "huge loss" you described.

3 · Why >50% win rate is still the wrong target

Forty simulated trades per style, same shape as before.
