The averaging trade isn’t even a disaster on this particular day — HDFC Bank is low-beta, the range is small. That’s the trap: it’s survivable often enough to feel like it works. The day it doesn’t (a gap-down open on bad news while you’re still holding the average) is the one that erases months of small wins — precisely the “huge loss” pattern you described.

3 · Why >50% win rate is still the wrong target

Forty simulated trades per style, same shape as the Hyundai example — the setup direction (long or short) doesn’t change the math. Averaging into every dip or spike wins 70% of the time (+0.4R average) but its losers average −1.8R, and twice per cycle a “this has to turn around” position runs to −5R or worse. The plan wins only 45% of the time and still finishes ahead, because every loss is capped at −1R and every win is +2R.
